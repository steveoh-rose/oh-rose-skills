# Case study template

```markdown
# [Project name]

[One sentence: what it was and what changed. Facts only.]

Role: [what you did]
Team: [roles]
Timeframe: [dates or duration]
Tools: [only what was actually used]

## Context
[Company and product. What the team was trying to do. 2-4 short sentences.]

## Problem
[What was wrong, for whom, and why it mattered. Evidence if any, labelled honestly.]

## Decisions
### [Decision 1, stated plainly]
[What was chosen. Alternatives considered. Why this one. Show the work.]

### [Decision 2]
[Same.]

## Trade-offs
[What was given up. Constraints: time, tech, people, data.]

## My contribution
[What I did directly. What the team did. No blur.]

## Result
[What changed. Real outcomes only. If nothing was measured, say what was observed and by whom.]

## What I would do next
[Optional. One or two honest sentences.]
```

## Prompts when facts are thin
- What was the situation before you started?
- Who was affected, and how did you know?
- What did you consider and reject?
- What was the hardest call, and what tipped it?
- What did you build, design or decide yourself?
- What changed afterwards, and how do you know?
- What would you change now?

## Checks
- Is the problem clear in the first screen?
- Could someone name two decisions after reading?
- Is every number sourced or a visible placeholder?
- Is contribution separate from team outcome?
- Is it more than a list of screens?
