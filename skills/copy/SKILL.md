# Copy

Direct, specific, human, quietly confident. Let evidence do the talking.

## Rules

- Concrete over corporate. Short paragraphs. Useful headings.
- Sentence case. No all-caps styling.
- No em dashes. Use a full stop, comma, colon or parentheses.
- Labels describe outcomes: "Read the case study", not "Learn more".
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it.
- No hype, filler openers, or "every project was transformative".
- Avoid: crafting delightful experiences, pixel-perfect, bridging the gap, passionate about, leverage, seamless.
- Never invent metrics, outcomes, clients or quotes. Use a visible placeholder like `[add result]` and say so.
- Separate personal contribution from team outcome.

## Case studies

Context, problem, decisions and alternatives, trade-offs, your contribution, result. Explain thinking. Do not list deliverables.

## Process

1. Read nearby text and match its voice.
2. Name what the reader should understand or do.
3. Write the shortest honest version. Cut what does not serve that.
4. Scan for em dashes, caps, vague labels, unsupported claims.

## Output

The text, then a list of placeholders and claims to confirm.
