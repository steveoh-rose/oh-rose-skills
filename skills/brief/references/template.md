# Brief template

Copy, fill in, delete sections that add nothing.

```markdown
# [Working title]

## Context
What is happening, in my own words:
[2-4 sentences]

Known:
- [fact]
Unknowns:
- [question]
Constraints:
- [time, tech, content, approvals]
Assumptions (unvalidated):
- [assumption]

## Problem
Outcome: [one sentence, specific and observable]
Who it is for: [user group]
Why now: [trigger, deadline, cost of waiting]

## Opportunities
How might we...
- [HMW 1]
- [HMW 2]
- [HMW 3]

Paths to success:
1. [Path A]
2. [Path B]
3. [Path C, the unconventional one]

## Impact
Riskiest assumptions:
1. [assumption] | cheapest test: [method]
2. [assumption] | cheapest test: [method]

Also affected:
- [page, component, flow, content]

Contributors and decision makers:
- [person or role]: [what they decide]

## Measures
Progress (while building):
- [signal]
Success (after release):
- [outcome]

## Next step
[One thing to do first]
```

## Notes

- Label evidence: user evidence, proxy evidence, stakeholder opinion, or assumption.
- Only list measures you can actually observe. Check what analytics exist before promising any.
- If you cannot fill Problem in one sentence, stop and ask a question.
- Ask for missing context instead of guessing: who the users are, what exists today, what the deadline is, what has already been tried.
