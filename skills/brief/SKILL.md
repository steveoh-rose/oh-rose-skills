# Brief

First decide: task or project?

- **Task.** Known problem, existing pattern, easy to undo. Skip the brief. Say so and execute.
- **Project.** High uncertainty, complex workflow, or 0-to-1. Write the brief.

## Brief (one page)

**Context.** What is happening, in your own words. Unknowns, constraints, assumptions.
**Problem.** The specific outcome, who it is for, why now. One sentence for the outcome.
**Opportunities.** Three "how might we" questions. Two or three different paths to success.
**Impact.** Riskiest unvalidated assumptions and the cheapest test for each. What else is affected. Who decides.
**Measures.** Progress signals while building. One success outcome after release.

End with one line: the first thing to do.

## Rules

- Label each claim fact, assumption or open question.
- Never invent research, numbers or quotes.
- Offer at least two paths. Do not anchor on the first idea.
- If the problem will not fit in one sentence, ask a question instead of writing more.
- Ask for missing context (users, what exists today, deadline, what was tried) rather than guessing.
